Agent [Small talk]: Grief is the emotional response to loss — basically your mind and body adjusting to something (or someone) important being gone.

**How it feels:**
- Heavy sadness, sometimes coming in sudden waves that hit out of nowhere
- Numbness or feeling disconnected, like things aren't real
- Physical stuff — tight chest, fatigue, trouble sleeping or eating, low energy
- Guilt or "what if" thints on loop
- Anger, sometimes at the situation, sometimes at the person who's gone, sometimes at yourself
- Occasionally relief, especially after a long illness or difficult relationship — and then guilt about feeling relieved

**How it sounds like (in someone's words or behavior):**
- "I keep expecting them to walk in" or "It doesn't feel real yet"
- Going quiet, withdrawing from people
- Getting irritated more easily over small things
- Talking about the person/situation a lot — or avoiding the topic entirely
- Trouble concentrating, forgetting things, feeling foggy

**How to know if someone's in grief:**
- Noticeable change from their usual self — quieter, more tired, more withdrawn, or more irritable
- Trouble with daily routines — work, sleep, eating
- They mention the loss often, or very specifically avoid mentioning it
- Crying more easily, or seeming flat/numb when they'd normally react
- It doesn't follow a neat timeline — someone can seem "fine" for weeks and then be hit hard again
